From discovery to decision

How to use a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet helps you organize possible purchases. The useful next step is checking the exact listing, chosen variant and total cost.

01

Find and identify

Filter by product type, then open the source listing. Save the item ID, seller URL, size, color and quantity. Similar pictures do not establish that two listings contain the same product.

02

Check fit and evidence

Compare the seller’s measurements with a garment or shoe that fits you. Distinguish listing images from warehouse QC photos. Ask for the measurements or detail photos you need before approving an item.

03

Confirm the buying route

These product links open HipobuyFind. If you intend to use LoveGoBuy, obtain the underlying marketplace link and check whether your agent accepts that item. Review the actual order summary before payment.

Know what you are adding up

Build a CNY budget

Enter your own quotes to combine product costs, seller delivery, agent charges, international shipping and a reserve for other costs.

Use amounts in CNY throughout. Convert any foreign-currency quote first. This tool adds your inputs; it does not retrieve freight rates, calculate taxes or produce an agent quote.

Useful answers

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list used to collect and compare potential purchases. This page provides a searchable selection from HipobuyFind alongside a buying checklist. It does not operate a LoveGoBuy account, checkout or warehouse service.

Where do the products on this page come from?

The eight product records, images, item IDs and CNY reference prices were taken from the public HipobuyFind catalog on 19 November 2026. Open each source listing to review its current information. This selection is not a live feed or the entire catalog.

Do the two main buttons open LoveGoBuy?

No. “LoveGoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” both open https://hipobuyfind.com. The product cards open individual HipobuyFind listings. Review the buying options there before proceeding.

Are these live prices or final delivered totals?

No. They are catalog reference prices in CNY from the source-check date. The chosen option may cost differently. Delivery, service charges, currency conversion and other applicable costs can change the amount you pay.

Are the product images QC photos?

They are the images displayed in the source catalog. We have not independently verified them as warehouse QC photos. For an order, request evidence for your actual item and check its size, color, condition and relevant measurements.

Can every listed item be ordered through LoveGoBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Obtain the original marketplace URL and check it with your chosen agent. A catalog entry does not guarantee that a platform can purchase or ship that item.

What should I do if a product is unavailable?

Keep the source item ID and product name, then look for another current listing. Compare the replacement’s seller, measurements, options and photos again rather than assuming it is identical.
